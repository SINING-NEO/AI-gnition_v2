The path

1. OrdersFictional purchases. Gaps are the days between consecutive orders.
2. Timing modelRidge regression predicts the next gap in days.
3. Product modelSoftmax picks the next product and a confidence.
4. Stock and offerKeep the prediction only if that product is in stock.
5. PolicyConsent, history, cooldown, and the due window. Not the model.
6. Holdout20% of eligible people are kept back for measurement.
7. Rank and capScore the rest. Keep the top 250.
8. Review queueA draft and a landing link. A person still has to approve a send.

Where the 3,000 people went

These counts partition the scored file. They come from prediction/campaign_output/campaign_summary.json.

1,127 people were inside a contact window before the holdout and the cap: 250 queued + 209 holdout + 668 over the 250-person limit. The queued 250 are all in the lapsed state, because a lapsed customer scores higher than a due-soon customer.

Formulas the code actually uses

gap_i = order_date_i − order_date_(i−1) in days mean = (1 / n) × Σ gap_i median = middle value of the sorted gaps std = √( (1 / n) × Σ (gap_i − mean)² ) population standard deviation z_j = (x_j − training_mean_j) / training_scale_j predicted_gap = clip( bias + z · weights , 7 , 180 ) days_until_due = predicted_gap − days_since_last_purchase P_k = exp(logit_k − max(logit)) / Σ exp(logit_j − max(logit)) opportunity = 0.5 × state_weight + 0.3 × product_confidence + 0.2 × dominant_product_share state_weight = 1.0 if lapsed, else 0.8 MAE = (1 / n) × Σ | actual_gap − predicted_gap | holdout bucket = integer( first 8 hex digits of SHA-256(customer_id) ) mod 100 holdout if bucket < 20

Sources: prediction/train_models.py and prediction/score_customers.py. Test MAE and accuracy are from prediction/artifacts/metrics.json: personal-median baseline 10.19 days, ridge 8.193 days, repeat-latest-product baseline 49.09%, softmax 77.94%.
