Frequently asked questions

MVP demonstration: answers are fictional, approved test content. Unsupported questions should be transferred to a human.

Why did I receive this recommendation?

The demo uses your fictional purchase timing and product history, then checks consent, current stock and active offers. It does not place an order automatically. Read the recommendation policy.

Is the recommended product definitely available?

The displayed stock comes from the latest synthetic inventory snapshot and is checked again at checkout. A recommendation does not reserve stock. Read the stock policy.

How do I know the discount is valid?

The prototype only displays an offer when the current date falls between its approved start and end dates. The checkout price remains final. Read the promotion policy.

How long does delivery take?

Standard demo delivery within Singapore is estimated at three to five business days. Read the delivery policy.

Can I return an item?

Unopened and unused products may be returned within 14 calendar days with proof of purchase. Different rules apply to damaged, incorrect or opened consumable items. Read the return policy.

How do I stop marketing messages?

Reply “stop” in the conversation. The workflow should suppress future marketing messages and record the preference.

Can I speak to a person?

Yes. Select “Talk to a person” at any time. Unsupported, sensitive or disputed enquiries should also be handed over automatically. Read the handover policy.
